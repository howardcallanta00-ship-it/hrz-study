# Effective Communication - Term 2 Mastery Question Bank

## Questions

### Multiple Choice

1. Which description best captures an interview?
   - A. A communication process for exchanging or sharing information involving an individual
   - B. A formal event in which experts address a large audience
   - C. A digital exchange that continues through ongoing posts
   - D. A written argument intended to persuade an audience

2. An interviewer uses the same prepared questions for every participant and allows little flexibility. Which type is being used?
   - A. Unstructured interview
   - B. Semi-structured interview
   - C. Structured interview
   - D. Roundtable interview

3. Which practice best represents an unstructured interview?
   - A. Asking only standardized questions in a fixed order
   - B. Asking any relevant questions that help achieve the interview goal
   - C. Combining a fixed questionnaire with no follow-up questions
   - D. Inviting experts to answer questions before an audience

4. What makes a semi-structured interview distinct?
   - A. It gives complete freedom with no planned questions.
   - B. It uses standardized questions with no flexibility.
   - C. It is conducted synchronously or asynchronously online.
   - D. It combines features of structured and unstructured interviews.

5. Which sequence correctly presents the three-part interview structure?
   - A. Before, during, after
   - B. Hook, background, thesis
   - C. Research, outline, write
   - D. Introduce, moderate, summarize

6. During a feature interview, a partner becomes hesitant when asked about a private matter. What should the interviewer do?
   - A. Ask the question again using stronger wording.
   - B. Explain that full disclosure is required.
   - C. Respect the boundary immediately and pivot.
   - D. Continue because the topic may attract attention.

7. Which goal follows the Golden Rule for an ethical feature writer?
   - A. Expose secrets that make the story dramatic
   - B. Highlight the partner's strengths
   - C. Challenge every boundary to obtain details
   - D. Prioritize controversy over the partner's welfare

8. Which set best states the primary focus of small-group communication?
   - A. Sharing ideas or stories, planning projects, and solving problems
   - B. Delivering expert opinions to a large audience only
   - C. Building personal relationships as the main priority
   - D. Producing visual and auditory modes for an essay

9. What factors influence the structure of small-group communication?
   - A. The title, thesis, and final departure point
   - B. The speaking time, slides, and audience rules only
   - C. The members' composition, background, and overall group goal
   - D. The number of citations and major details in the script

10. In which setting does leadership arise naturally from a member's confidence or knowledge to participate?
   - A. Centralized setting
   - B. Informal setting
   - C. Formal setting
   - D. Panel setting

11. Which feature most clearly identifies a formal small-group setting?
   - A. Members post whenever they choose.
   - B. Leadership emerges only from confidence.
   - C. All participants speak without organization.
   - D. A facilitator organizes and monitors engagement.

12. A group wants every participant to have an equal opportunity to engage in discussion. Which organization fits best?
   - A. Decentralized
   - B. Centralized
   - C. Panel-based
   - D. Unstructured

13. When is a centralized structure described as especially efficient?
   - A. When the group has no specific goal
   - B. When participation must have no leader
   - C. When achieving a specific outcome such as a project or resolution
   - D. When posts must continue asynchronously

14. Which description matches a roundtable discussion?
   - A. A formal event of experts before a large audience
   - B. An informal circle of ideally 8-15 members with a non-dominating facilitator
   - C. A digital format using synchronous or asynchronous posts
   - D. A single leader directing a group toward a resolution

15. Which situation is a panel discussion?
   - A. Experts discuss a topic at a formal event before a large audience.
   - B. Eight members sit in a circle and share equally.
   - C. Participants exchange ongoing posts on a digital platform.
   - D. One student interviews a partner using standard questions.

16. What distinguishes an online forum from the other discussion formats?
   - A. It must have exactly 8-15 members.
   - B. It requires experts before a live audience.
   - C. It excludes a facilitator or moderator.
   - D. It can operate synchronously or asynchronously through ongoing posts.

17. Which duty belongs primarily to the moderator or facilitator?
   - A. Prepare speech outlines and slides as a panelist
   - B. Research the topic only to ask an audience question
   - C. Explain guidelines and enforce speaking-time limits
   - D. Speak only when called upon by another participant

18. Which combination is expected of a panelist?
   - A. Set audience rules, introduce participants, and enforce time limits
   - B. Prepare an outline and slides, observe time limits, and avoid interrupting
   - C. Research the topic, wait for a turn, and keep posts on topic
   - D. Arrange members in a circle and monitor equal participation

19. Which behavior best fulfills the audience role?
   - A. Research beforehand, listen attentively, and wait for a turn to speak
   - B. Control the speaking time of every participant
   - C. Prepare the panelists' speech outlines and slides
   - D. Call on speakers and introduce every participant

20. Which audience behavior violates the stated expectations?
   - A. Keeping comments on topic
   - B. Listening attentively
   - C. Waiting for a turn to speak
   - D. Using foul or biased language

21. Why is a video essay an example of multimodal communication?
   - A. It relies only on written evidence and citations.
   - B. It combines visual modes and auditory modes.
   - C. It uses only voiceover and background music.
   - D. It presents only videos, images, and text.

22. Which pairing contains one visual mode and one auditory mode from a video essay?
   - A. Image and text
   - B. Voiceover and music
   - C. Video and voiceover
   - D. Text and image

23. Which elements belong in the introduction of a video-essay script?
   - A. Hook, cited background information, and clear thesis
   - B. Counterargument, refutation, and emphatic sentence
   - C. Restated thesis, summary, and final challenge
   - D. Major details, minor details, and closing quotation

24. The body of a video-essay script should support how many major points?
   - A. 1-2
   - B. 2-3
   - C. 4-6
   - D. 3-5

25. Which set consists of minor-detail forms listed for the body of a video essay?
   - A. Facts, evidence, statistics, and examples
   - B. Hook, thesis, quotation, and challenge
   - C. Pro stance, con stance, title, and tone
   - D. Background, refutation, recommendation, and outline

26. Which is an appropriate final departure point for a video essay?
   - A. A new unsupported major point
   - B. A call to action, quotation, or challenge
   - C. A list of standardized interview questions
   - D. A switch from the thesis to an unrelated issue

27. Which description best defines a position paper?
   - A. A digital discussion conducted through ongoing posts
   - B. A script that combines visual and auditory modes
   - C. An academic genre presenting an evidence-based argument to persuade an audience
   - D. A standardized exchange with little room for flexibility

28. In CROW, what does the letter C require first?
   - A. Choose a debatable issue.
   - B. Cite every minor detail.
   - C. Challenge the final audience.
   - D. Combine visual and auditory modes.

29. Which thesis satisfies the C step as described?
   - A. A statement that merely reports an uncontested fact
   - B. A list of sources without a position
   - C. A neutral title that avoids any issue
   - D. An arguable thesis supporting a clear pro or con stance

30. Which action belongs to the R step in CROW?
   - A. Restate the thesis in the conclusion.
   - B. Gather information and use reliable references.
   - C. Refute the counterargument in the body.
   - D. Write the full draft using proper tone.

31. Which sequence matches the keyhole structure used when outlining a position paper?
   - A. Thesis, hook, evidence; arguments; new issue
   - B. Background, recommendation; slides; quotation
   - C. Hook/background/thesis; arguments/evidence/counterargument/refutation; restated thesis/emphatic sentence/final action
   - D. Standard questions; flexible questions; audience questions

32. Where do the counterargument and refutation belong in the position-paper outline?
   - A. Body
   - B. Title
   - C. Introduction
   - D. Conclusion

33. What does the W step of CROW direct the writer to do?
   - A. Select either a pro or con position only
   - B. Draft the complete paper from the outline using proper language, tone, and logic
   - C. Collect reliable references without writing
   - D. Arrange the hook, background, and thesis only

34. What is the purpose of WAUR?
   - A. To assign speaking roles in a panel
   - B. To classify structured and unstructured interviews
   - C. To list the stages before, during, and after an interview
   - D. To guide effective language choices for clear, forceful, persuasive arguments

35. Which expansion of WAUR is correct?
   - A. Writing either Pro or Con; Avoiding fallacies; Using modals; Refuting counterarguments
   - B. Writing an outline; Adding facts; Using references; Restating conclusions
   - C. Watching evidence; Asking questions; Understanding roles; Reporting results
   - D. Writing a thesis; Arranging slides; Using music; Revising narration

### True or False

36. A structured interview allows the interviewer broad freedom to ask any relevant question that may help achieve the goal.

37. In a roundtable discussion, the facilitator should keep track of the exchange without dominating it.

38. A centralized discussion gives every participant equal opportunity by removing the single leader or facilitator.

39. Audience members are expected to research the topic in advance and avoid biased language.

40. The closing of a video essay should introduce three to five new major points.

41. A position paper aims to show that the writer's evidence-based perspective deserves consideration.

42. In CROW, research includes reading, taking notes, and using reliable references to support the thesis.

43. WAUR treats the use of modals and the refutation of counterarguments as principles of language use.
